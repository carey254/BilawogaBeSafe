BilaWoga Safety App - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2025

IMPORTANT: This app is designed for emergency situations. Your safety is our priority.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

  • Name: Your name for emergency identification
  • Emergency Contacts: Phone numbers of trusted contacts for emergency situations
  • Location Data: GPS coordinates when SOS is activated
  • Audio Recordings: Audio is recorded when AI detects an emergency or when you manually send an SOS (1 second to 10 minutes)

1.2 Technical Information

  • Device Information: Basic device details for app functionality
  • App Usage: SOS activation times and frequency (for abuse prevention)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Emergency Response: Send your location and emergency message to your trusted contacts
  • Audio Recording: Record and transmit audio evidence to your trusted contacts during emergencies
  • AI Emergency Detection: Monitor background audio to detect emergency sounds (screaming, distress calls, cries for help)
  • App Functionality: Enable shake detection and SOS features
  • Security: Prevent app misuse and ensure legitimate emergency use

3. Data Security

Security Measures:
  • All data is stored locally on your device using AES-256-GCM encryption
  • Location data is only shared when you activate the SOS feature
  • Audio recordings are sent only to your trusted emergency contacts
  • Optional encrypted cloud backup is available to restore your data if you reinstall or log back in
  • No data is shared with third parties without your explicit consent

4. Audio Recording (RECORD_AUDIO Permission)

Why We Record Audio:

BilaWoga uses the RECORD_AUDIO permission to:

  • AI Emergency Detection: Monitor background audio in real-time to detect emergency sounds (screaming, distress calls, cries for help)
  • Emergency Evidence: Record audio (1 second to 10 minutes) when an emergency is detected or when you manually send an SOS
  • Voice Learning: Learn and identify voices to improve emergency detection accuracy

When Audio is Recorded:

  • Only when AI detects a potential emergency situation
  • Only when you manually activate the SOS feature
  • Audio is recorded for 1 second to 10 minutes, depending on the situation

Who Receives Audio Recordings:

  • Audio recordings are sent ONLY to your trusted emergency contacts
  • Audio is transmitted via SMS/MMS or WhatsApp to your contacts
  • Audio is NOT stored on any external servers
  • Audio is NOT shared with third parties

5. Stealth Mode & Silent Logging

Your Safety is Our Priority:

BilaWoga uses Stealth Mode to protect you during emergencies. When you send an SOS alert, there is no visible indication on your device - no messages, notifications, or confirmations appear. This ensures your safety by preventing detection.

5.1 How Stealth Mode Works

  • Silent SOS Sending: When you activate SOS (manually, by shaking your device, or via AI detection), the message is sent directly to your trusted contacts without any visible indication on your phone
  • No SMS App UI: Messages are sent directly to the carrier network, bypassing SMS apps entirely
  • Automatic Audio Recording: When SOS is activated, the app automatically records audio and sends it to your trusted contacts
  • Automatic Retry: If sending fails, the app automatically retries up to 5 times to ensure delivery

5.2 Silent Logging System

All emergency events are logged securely and silently for your review when safe:

  • Encrypted Storage: All logs are stored in encrypted format on your device using AES-256-GCM encryption
  • What is Logged: SOS attempts, success/failure status, audio recording status, retry attempts, and delivery confirmations
  • Privacy Protected: Phone numbers in logs are masked (e.g., +2****56) for privacy
  • No Network Sharing: Logs never leave your device - they are stored locally only

5.3 How to View Your Emergency Logs

To check if your SOS was sent successfully:

  1. Open the BilaWoga app
  2. Tap the menu icon (⋮) in the top-right corner
  3. Select "View Activity Log" or go to "Settings"
  4. View your emergency logs to see:
    • EMERGENCY_SOS_SUCCESS - Message sent successfully
    • EMERGENCY_AUDIO_SENT - Audio recording sent
    • EMERGENCY_SOS_RETRY_SUCCESS - Retry successful
    • EMERGENCY_SOS_FAILED - Send failed (will retry automatically)

Important: Logs are only stored on your device. You can review them when safe to verify that your SOS was sent and received by your trusted contacts.

6. Data Sharing

We do NOT share your data with:

  • Third-party advertisers
  • Data brokers
  • Analytics companies (except Firebase Analytics for app improvement, which you can opt-out of)
  • Any external parties without your explicit consent

We ONLY share data with:

  • Your trusted emergency contacts (when you activate SOS)
  • Emergency services (if you choose to contact them directly)

7. Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all your stored data within the app
  • Deletion: You can delete all your data at any time through the app settings
  • Control: You control which emergency contacts receive your information
  • Opt-out: You can disable AI monitoring and audio recording features at any time

8. How to Request Data Deletion

Requesting Data Deletion from BilaWoga Safety App

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data at any time. BilaWoga provides an easy in-app method to delete all your data.

8.1 In-App Data Deletion (Recommended)

Follow these steps to delete all your data:

  1. Open the BilaWoga Safety App on your device
  2. Tap the menu icon (⋮) in the top-right corner of the screen
  3. Select "Logout" from the menu
  4. You will see a dialog with two options:
    • "Erase now" - This will completely delete all your data immediately
    • "Back up to cloud & log out" - This backs up your data to cloud first, then deletes local data
  5. Select "Erase now" to permanently delete all your data
  6. Confirm the deletion when prompted
  7. You will see a confirmation message: "Data erased and logged out"
  8. The app will return to the onboarding screen, indicating all data has been deleted

8.2 What Data is Deleted

When you select "Erase now", the following data is permanently deleted:

  • Your name - Personal identification information
  • Emergency contacts - Phone numbers of trusted contacts
  • Incident type preferences - Your selected emergency incident type
  • Audio recordings - All locally stored audio files
  • Location history - Any stored location data
  • App settings - All app configuration and preferences
  • User identity - Your unique user ID (prevents data restoration)
  • Encrypted storage - All data stored in encrypted SharedPreferences
  • Session data - All temporary app session information

8.3 What Data is NOT Deleted

The following data may remain:

  • Cloud backups (if created): If you previously selected "Back up to cloud & log out", your data may be stored in Firebase. However, without your User ID (which is deleted), this data cannot be restored or accessed.
  • Emergency messages already sent: SMS messages and audio recordings that were already sent to your emergency contacts cannot be deleted, as they are stored on your contacts' devices and carrier networks.

8.4 Data Retention Period

Immediate Deletion: When you select "Erase now", all data is deleted immediately and permanently. There is no retention period - your data is removed from your device instantly.

No Recovery: Once data is deleted using "Erase now", it cannot be recovered. The User ID is also removed, preventing any cloud backup restoration.

8.5 Alternative: Uninstall the App

If you prefer, you can also delete all your data by uninstalling the BilaWoga Safety App from your device. Uninstalling the app will remove all locally stored data.

Note: Uninstalling does not delete any cloud backups that may have been created. To ensure complete deletion, use the "Erase now" option before uninstalling.
