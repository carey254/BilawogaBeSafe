# How to Create a Privacy Policy URL (Step-by-Step)

## You Need to CREATE a URL - Here's How:

### ✅ Step 1: You Have the File
- File location: `privacy-policy.html` in your project folder
- File size: 10.25 KB
- This file contains your complete privacy policy

### ✅ Step 2: Upload It to Get a URL

## EASIEST METHOD: Google Sites (2 minutes)

1. **Open Google Sites:**
   - Go to: https://sites.google.com
   - Sign in with your Google account (or create one - it's free)

2. **Create New Site:**
   - Click the **"Create"** button (or the **"+"** icon)
   - Click **"New site"**

3. **Name Your Site:**
   - At the top, change the title to: **"BilaWoga Privacy Policy"**

4. **Add Your Privacy Policy:**
   - Click the **"+"** button on the page
   - Select **"Embed"** or **"Text"**
   - **Option A:** Upload the `privacy-policy.html` file
   - **Option B:** Open `privacy-policy.html` in Notepad, copy all the content, and paste it here

5. **Publish Your Site:**
   - Click **"Publish"** button (top right)
   - Choose a web address (e.g., `bilawoga-privacy-policy`)
   - Click **"Publish"** again

6. **Get Your URL:**
   - You'll see: `https://sites.google.com/view/bilawoga-privacy-policy`
   - **COPY THIS URL** - This is what you need!

---

## ALTERNATIVE: Netlify Drop (Even Easier - 30 seconds)

1. **Open Netlify Drop:**
   - Go to: https://app.netlify.com/drop
   - No account needed!

2. **Drag Your File:**
   - Open File Explorer
   - Navigate to: `C:\Users\User\OneDrive\Desktop\SAFTEY BILAWOGA APP IMPROVED\BilaWoga`
   - Drag the file `privacy-policy.html` into the Netlify Drop page

3. **Get Your URL:**
   - You'll instantly get a URL like: `https://random-name-123.netlify.app`
   - **COPY THIS URL** - This is what you need!

---

## ✅ Step 3: Add URL to Play Console

1. **Go to Play Console:**
   - https://play.google.com/console
   - Select your app: **Bila Woga**

2. **Find Privacy Policy Section:**
   - In left sidebar: **Policy** → **App content** → **Privacy policy**
   - OR search for "privacy policy" in the search bar

3. **Paste Your URL:**
   - Paste the URL you got from Google Sites or Netlify
   - Click **"Save"**

4. **Done!** ✅

---

## Summary:
1. ✅ You have the file: `privacy-policy.html`
2. ⏳ Upload it to Google Sites or Netlify (get a URL)
3. ⏳ Paste that URL in Play Console

**The URL doesn't exist yet - you CREATE it by uploading the file!**
