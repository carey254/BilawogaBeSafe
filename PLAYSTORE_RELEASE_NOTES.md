# Play Store Release Notes

## Recommended Release Notes (English)

```
🎉 Welcome to BilaWoga - Your Personal Safety Companion

BilaWoga is a comprehensive emergency safety app designed to protect you when you need help most. This initial release includes powerful features to keep you safe:

✨ Key Features:
• Emergency SOS with Stealth Mode - Send emergency alerts silently without any visible indication on your device
• AI-Powered Emergency Detection - Advanced AI automatically detects emergency sounds and sends help
• Automatic Audio Recording - Records and sends audio evidence to your trusted contacts
• Shake Detection - Shake your device to instantly send an SOS alert
• Secure Encrypted Storage - All your data is encrypted and stored securely on your device
• Multi-Language Support - Available in English and Swahili
• Trusted Contact Verification - Only sends to verified trusted contacts for maximum security

🔒 Privacy & Security:
• All data stored locally on your device
• AES-256 encryption for sensitive information
• No data shared without your permission
• Stealth mode ensures your safety during emergencies

📱 How It Works:
1. Set up your emergency contacts
2. The app monitors for emergencies in the background
3. When danger is detected, SOS is sent automatically with your location
4. Your trusted contacts receive the alert with audio recording

⚠️ Important: This app is designed for genuine emergency situations only. Please use responsibly.

Thank you for choosing BilaWoga. Your safety is our priority.
```

## What to Include in Play Store

1. **Release name:** `1.0`
2. **Release notes:** Use one of the versions above (English recommended)
3. **What's new in this version:** (For first release, same as release notes)

### Optional but Recommended:
- **App description:** (Already in store listing)
- **Screenshots:** Add screenshots showing key features
- **Feature graphic:** Add a banner image
