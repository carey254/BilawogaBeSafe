🎉 Initial Release of BilaWoga Safety App

BilaWoga helps protect you during emergencies with powerful safety features:

• Emergency SOS with Stealth Mode
  Send emergency alerts silently without any visible indication on your device. Only your trusted contacts receive the alert, ensuring your safety during dangerous situations.

• AI-Powered Emergency Detection
  Advanced AI automatically monitors background audio to detect emergency sounds (screaming, distress calls, cries for help). When detected, SOS is sent automatically with your location and audio recording.

• Automatic Audio Recording
  Records audio (1 second to 10 minutes) when emergencies are detected and sends it to your trusted contacts as evidence.

• Shake Detection
  Shake your device to instantly send an SOS alert with your location. Works even when the app is locked or in the background.

• Secure Encrypted Storage
  All sensitive data is encrypted using AES-256 and stored securely on your device. Your privacy is our priority.

• Multi-Language Support
  Available in English and Swahili for wider accessibility.

• Trusted Contact Verification
  SOS messages are only sent to verified trusted contacts stored in encrypted storage, ensuring maximum security.

All your data is stored securely on your device. This app is for genuine emergency situations only.

Thank you for choosing BilaWoga. Your safety is our priority.
