# How to Add Privacy Policy URL in Google Play Console

## Where to Find It in Play Console

### Method 1: Direct Navigation Path
1. Go to **Google Play Console**: https://play.google.com/console
2. Select your app: **Bila Woga**
3. In the left sidebar, look for:
   - **Policy** → **App content** → **Privacy policy**
   OR
   - **Store presence** → **App content** → **Privacy policy**
   OR
   - **Content rating** → **Privacy policy** (sometimes here)

### Method 2: Search in Play Console
1. Click the **search icon** (magnifying glass) at the top
2. Type: **"privacy policy"**
3. Click on the result that says **"Privacy policy"**

### Method 3: Step-by-Step Navigation
1. **Play Console** → Your App
2. Left sidebar menu:
   - Expand **"Policy"** section
   - Click **"App content"**
   - Scroll down to find **"Privacy policy"**
   - Click **"Privacy policy"**
3. You'll see a field: **"Privacy policy URL"**
4. Enter your URL and click **"Save"**

## If You Can't Find It

The location might vary based on your Play Console version. Try these:
- **Policy** → **Privacy policy**
- **Store presence** → **Privacy policy**
- **App content** → **Privacy policy**
- **Content rating** → **Privacy policy**

## Hosting Your Privacy Policy (Free Options)

Since you don't have a website, here are FREE options:

### Option 1: GitHub Pages (Recommended - Free & Easy)
1. Go to https://github.com
2. Create a free account (if you don't have one)
3. Create a new repository (e.g., "bilawoga-privacy")
4. Create a file: `index.html`
5. Copy the privacy policy HTML from your app
6. Enable GitHub Pages in repository settings
7. Your URL will be: `https://yourusername.github.io/bilawoga-privacy/`

### Option 2: Google Sites (Free & Very Easy)
1. Go to https://sites.google.com
2. Click **"Create"** → **"New site"**
3. Name it: "BilaWoga Privacy Policy"
4. Paste your privacy policy content
5. Click **"Publish"**
6. Your URL will be: `https://sites.google.com/view/bilawoga-privacy-policy`

### Option 3: Firebase Hosting (Free)
1. Go to https://firebase.google.com
2. Create a project
3. Enable **Hosting**
4. Upload your HTML file
5. Your URL will be: `https://yourproject.web.app/privacy-policy.html`

### Option 4: Netlify Drop (Easiest - Just Drag & Drop)
1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Create a simple HTML file with your privacy policy
3. Drag and drop the file
4. Get instant URL: `https://random-name-123.netlify.app`
